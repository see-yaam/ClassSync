Everything Your Classroom Needs — All in One Place

ClassSync is built to give educators and students everything a modern classroom needs, all in one place. Whether you are an instructor managing dozens of students or a learner tracking your own academic progress, ClassSync provides the tools to make education more efficient, transparent, and engaging.

Instructors can create private or public classrooms, assign homework with deadlines, attach reference materials, and review every submission through an organized matrix view. Teaching assistants can be added to share the workload, and course pricing can be configured for free or paid enrollment.

Students benefit from real-time leaderboards, instant AI-powered auto-evaluation feedback, live video class sessions, and smart alerts from instructors. ClassSync brings together classroom management, homework tracking, video conferencing, automated grading, and performance analytics — everything your classroom needs, all in one place.
